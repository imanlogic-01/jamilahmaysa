Dance with Jamilah near Banora Point

Banora Point sits within Jamilah’s Tweed-area reach and connects naturally with classes and events around Tweed Heads and the coast.

Jamilah’s sessions are built around music, movement and community. You can join for fitness, confidence, dance skills or simply a better-feeling way to move your body.

What you can expect

Depending on the live timetable and current pop-ups, Jamilah’s class mix includes Dance Fit, Zumba, Afro Fusion and beginner-friendly sessions. Events and private group experiences are also available across the wider border region.

New to dance fitness?

You do not need dance experience to get started. Follow the room, take the steps at your pace and build confidence as you go. The focus is on movement and enjoyment rather than perfection.
