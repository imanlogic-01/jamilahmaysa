The person behind the Jam

MUSIC. MOVEMENT. COMMUNITY.

Jamilah Maysa is the instructor and creative energy behind Jamilah's Jam.

Her public dance work spans Dance Fit, Afro Fusion, Zumba, beginner sessions, pop-ups, community events, performance collaborations and social dance content. The common thread is simple: people should leave feeling better than when they arrived.

Her teaching style is expressive, energetic and welcoming. The goal is not to make every person in the room move identically. It is to help people find the rhythm, build confidence and enjoy moving.

The philosophy

LIFE IS RHYTHM.

Move

Use dance as a way to get active, release stress and reconnect with your body.

Connect

Build a room where different ages, backgrounds and experience levels can enjoy themselves together.

Express

Dance is not about getting every count right. It is about personality, music and finding your own groove.
