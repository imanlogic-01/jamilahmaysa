QUESTIONS? START HERE.

The things people usually want to know before their first class or before booking Jamilah for an event.

Book JamilahFind a class
Do I need dance experience?

No. Beginner-friendly options are part of the Jamilah's Jam offer. You can follow at your level and build confidence over time.

What should I bring to class?

Comfortable clothes, supportive shoes, water and enough room in your head to stop worrying about being perfect.

Can I book Jamilah for a private party?

Yes. Private celebrations, friendship groups, birthdays and hens-style events can be discussed through the gig enquiry page.

Does Jamilah do corporate events?

Yes. Corporate wellbeing, team-building and event energiser enquiries are welcomed.

Can Jamilah work with councils or community organisations?

Community programmes, festivals, fundraisers and public event enquiries are part of the commercial offer.

Can I book performance or choreography work?

Jamilah can discuss selected performance, choreography, creative shoot and music-led projects based on the brief and availability.

Where are classes held?

Jamilah teaches and collaborates across the Tweed Coast and Southern Gold Coast. Because class schedules change, the timetable page links to the latest live announcements.
