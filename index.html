More than a class

COME FOR THE DANCE.
STAY FOR THE PEOPLE.

Jamilah's Jam is movement, music, confidence and connection. No audition. No pressure to be perfect. Turn up, catch the rhythm and make the hour yours.

Jamilah blends dance fitness with Afro, Latin, hip-hop and pop influences. Weekly classes are one part of the brand. Jamilah is also available for private groups, corporate wellbeing, festivals, community events, workshops and performance-led experiences.
