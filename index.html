Welcome to the Jam

COME FOR THE DANCE.
STAY FOR THE PEOPLE.

Jamilah's Jam is built around movement, music, confidence and connection. The room is for people who want to move their body, learn the steps and enjoy themselves without the pressure to be perfect.

Led by Jamilah Maysa, the experience blends dance fitness with Afro, Latin, hip-hop and pop influences. Some people come for the workout. Some come for the music. Most come back for the energy.

No audition. No judgement.

BEGIN WHERE YOU ARE.

You do not need to be a dancer to walk into the room. Start with the basic step, catch the rhythm, laugh when it goes wrong and keep moving.

Jamilah's classes are designed to feel welcoming, social and alive. You can come for fitness, confidence, dance skills or simply because you need an hour that feels like yours.
